## 1. The product (one paragraph)

WhatsApp-first project organisation and change-approval platform for Indian residential
interiors/construction. Three parties per project: the **designer/architect** (primary customer;
the project lives in their workspace; only they change the record), the **construction company**
(collaborator; raises requests and sends files, incl. over WhatsApp), and the **homeowner**
(no-account portal link; approves/declines/sends back each change). Requests flow construction →
designer → homeowner. Evidence machinery (frozen hashed versions, append-only audit chain, PDFs)
is unchanged and a side benefit; organisation and low friction come first.
